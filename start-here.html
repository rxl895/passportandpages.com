Start with four.

There's no right order — nothing here is a series. But if you've just arrived and want to know whether this journal is for you, these are the pages I'd hand you first.

How this works

Three ways in

However you like to browse, there's a door for it.

By map

Every country with a stamp is inked in on a world map. Click a pin and you land on that country's page — everything written there, in one place.

By date

The journal is everything, newest first, filterable by continent. Good if you'd rather read the way it was written.

By scrap

Field notes are the thoughts too small for a whole page — a sentence off the back of a boarding pass. The fastest way to get the tone.

What to expect

Slow, late, and honest.

I write a week or two after getting back, once a trip has settled into something with a shape. No sponsored stays, no ten-best lists, no hidden gems. Just what a place was like on an ordinary Tuesday, including the parts that went badly.
